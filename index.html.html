01 / DIL SE MAAFI

Galti meri thi, aur mujhe sach mein afsos hai.

Sabse pehle, dil se sorry. Maine tumse jis tarah baat ki, woh bilkul galat tha. Tum jaisi pyaari, samajhdaar aur soft-hearted insaan ko mere alfaaz se hurt hona pada, aur iska mujhe sach mein afsos hai. Tum izzat aur narmi se baat kiye jaane ki haqdar ho—hamesha.

Main apni galti ko kisi bahaane ke peeche nahi chhupaunga. Ek sorry se sab kuch turant theek ho jaaye, aisa main expect nahi karta. Bas chahta hoon ki tum meri maafi ko mere actions mein bhi dekho, kyunki tumhara bharosa mere liye important hai.

02 / KOI BAHAANA NAHI

Tumhari feelings mere liye important hain.

Tumhari feelings mere liye sach mein matter karti hain. Tumhara dil bahut narm hai, aur tum chhoti-chhoti baaton mein bhi kitni care dikha deti ho—ye baat mujhe hamesha achhi lagti hai. Mujhe tumhari baat samajhne ki koshish karni chahiye thi, na ki gusse mein aise words bolne chahiye the.

Main ye kehkar apni galti chhoti nahi karunga ki mera woh matlab nahi tha. Jo tumne mehsoos kiya, woh important hai. Agar kabhi tum khud baat karna chaho, toh main pehle tumhari poori baat sununga.

03 / TUMHARI EHMIYAT

Tum hamesha izzat aur pyaar se baat kiye jaane ke laayak ho.

Tumhari khoobsurati sirf tumhari pyaari si smile ya tumhari aankhon mein nahi, balki tumhare nature mein bhi hai. Tumhari aankhon mein jo softness hai, tumhari smile se jo warmth mehsoos hoti hai, aur jis tarah tum doosron ki fikr karti ho—ye sab tumhe mere liye bahut special banata hai. Tum waqai ek bahut achhi aur soft-hearted insaan ho.

Mujhe tumhari hasi, tumhari baatein aur tumhara woh pyaara sa andaaz bahut pasand hai. Lekin in tareefon ka matlab ye nahi ki tumhe meri galti bhoolni pade. Tumhari izzat aur tumhari feelings, meri kisi bhi khwahish se pehle aati hain.

04 / DOORI KE BAAVJOOD

Faasle hain, par respect kabhi kam nahi honi chahiye.

Door rehne ke bawajood tumhari chhoti si baat bhi mere din ko achha bana deti hai. Kabhi tumhari smile yaad aati hai, kabhi tumhari aankhein, aur kabhi woh pyaari si baatein. Lekin distance ya emotions—kuch bhi mere rude behaviour ka excuse nahi ho sakta.

Maine ye page apni baat kehne ke liye banaya hai, tumse reply lene ki zid karne ke liye nahi. Agar tumhe waqt ya space chahiye, toh main uski respect karunga. Tum apni marzi se, apne time par hi baat karna.

05 / KHUD MEIN BADLAAV

Sorry ke saath actions bhi zaroori hain.

Main chahta hoon ki main tumse sirf achhe mood mein hi nahi, balki disagreement ke waqt bhi pyaar aur respect se baat karun. Pehle tumhari baat dhyaan se sunna, gusse mein ruk jaana, aur hurtful words se bachna—ye sab mujhe apne behaviour mein laana hai.

Main bade-bade promises karke tumhe convince nahi karna chahta. Main bas itna chahta hoon ki waqt ke saath tumhe mere actions mein farq dikhe. Tum mere liye important ho, isliye mujhe apne aap ko behtar banana chahiye.

06 / KOI PRESSURE NAHI

Jitna waqt chahiye, utna lena.

Tumhe abhi mujhe maaf karna zaroori nahi hai. Tum naraz ho, hurt ho, ya filhaal baat nahi karna chahti—main tumhari feelings ko samajhne ki koshish karunga. Tumhe apni feelings chhupakar mujhe theek feel karwane ki zaroorat bilkul nahi.

Main tumse turant 'it's okay' sunne ki umeed nahi rakhunga. Tum jitna waqt lena chaho, le sakti ho. Tumhara sukoon aur tumhari boundaries mere liye important hain.

07 / EK CHHOTI SI KHWAHISH

Kaash waqt ke saath tumhara mann halka ho.

Dil se chahta hoon ki tumhare chehre ki woh pyaari si smile phir se waise hi bani rahe. Tumhari khushi dekhna mujhe achha lagta hai, lekin main ye bhi samajhta hoon ki tumhari khushi ki zimmedari tumhari hai—main tum par khush rehne ka pressure nahi daal sakta.

Agar kabhi tum baat karna chaho, toh main bina beech mein roke tumhari baat sununga. Main tumhe samajhna chahta hoon, apni safai dene ke liye nahi, balki isliye kyunki tumhari feelings mere liye sach mein matter karti hain.

08 / HUMARI YAADEIN

Achhe pal aaj bhi mere liye khaas hain.

Humari chhoti-chhoti yaadein mere dil ke kareeb hain—woh hasi-mazaak, random baatein aur tumhara pyaara sa andaaz. Kabhi tumhari smile yaad aati hai toh apne aap chehre par bhi smile aa jaati hai. Ye sab mere liye bahut precious hai.

Tumne jo care, time aur pyaar share kiya, uske liye thank you. Main un yaadon ko tum par koi haq jatane ke liye use nahi karunga. Main bas unke liye grateful hoon.

09 / MERA KHUD SE VAADA

Ab alfaaz soch-samajh kar chununga.

Main khud se ye vaada karna chahta hoon ki gusse ya frustration mein bhi tumse tameez aur pyaar se baat karunga. Tumhari baat ko lightly nahi lunga, aur agar kabhi disagreement hua toh use samajhdari se handle karunga.

Trust sirf sweet messages se nahi banta; woh roz ke behaviour se banta hai. Isliye main tumse meri baaton par turant yakeen karne ko nahi kahunga. Main apne actions se apni sincerity dikhane ki koshish karunga.

10 / JAANE SE PEHLE

Bas ek baar phir, dil se sorry.

Mujhe tumhari bahut si baatein pasand hain—tumhari aankhon ki masoomiyat, tumhari smile ki warmth, tumhara soft-hearted nature aur tumhara apno ke liye care karna. Tumhari apni ek alag si sweetness hai, jo tumhe tum banati hai. Aur haan, tumse galti ya narazgi ho sakti hai; phir bhi tum respect aur kindness deserve karti ho.

Maine tumhe hurt kiya, aur uske liye main dil se sorry hoon. Kaash main us waqt apne alfaaz sambhaal paata. Main tumhe kisi jawab, maafi ya faisle ke liye force nahi karunga. Bas itna chahta hoon ki tum hamesha khush, safe aur respected feel karo—chahe tum mujhse baat karo ya na karo.
